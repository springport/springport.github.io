Mamonya: Lantern Seek & Find

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Find your favorite part.

01

Follow the light

Only the lantern-lit area reveals where you can find and tap a Mamonya.

02

Look a little closer

Drag to move and pinch to zoom. Search corners you might have missed on your first look.

03

Bring a few useful tools

Move rocks with gloves or use a bell to help your search. Wrong taps cost time, so take care where you tap.

GET STARTED

A few steps to get started.

  1. Read the wish for the stage
  2. Explore with your lantern
  3. Find and tap the hidden Mamonya

Ema are wooden wish plaques found at Japanese shrines. Granting a wish here is part of the game’s story.
