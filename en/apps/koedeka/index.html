KOEDEKA!

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Find your favorite part.

01

Find the narrow winning margin

In Knockout Battle, beat the KING by 1–99 points. A lower or equal score, or a jump of 100 points or more, eliminates you.

02

Keep a relay going together

Beat the last successful score as a team. Reach ten successes before three misses end the run.

03

Make the prompts your own

Choose a prompt set or add your own phrases. Take turns on the same phone and enjoy the reactions around you.

GET STARTED

A few steps to get started.

  1. Gather 2–5 players and pick a mode
  2. Take turns with one phone
  3. Aim for a careful increase in volume

Play somewhere suitable, at a comfortable volume. Use the same phone and a similar microphone distance for everyone.
