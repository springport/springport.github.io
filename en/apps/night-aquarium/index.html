Omikuji: Fortune Aquarium

Unwind & explore · ANDROID APP

A daily fortune. A quiet moment by the water.

Draw a Japanese fortune, then watch the sea life you have collected. Build a small night aquarium, one discovery at a time.

View on Google Play ↗

For Android · Contains ads

Japanese & English UI · follows device language

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Find your favorite part.

01

A little ritual for your day

Omikuji are fortune slips traditionally drawn at Japanese shrines and temples. Tap or shake to reveal your daily luck, lucky color and a short message.

02

A collection that grows with you

Some draws bring a new creature. Save pearls from fortunes to welcome more of the 27 species, and choose which ones swim in your tank.

03

Stay for the aquarium

Watch your collection swim, drop food with a tap, and turn the background music on or off.

GET STARTED

A few steps to get started.

  1. Draw your daily fortune
  2. Read your message and collect pearls
  3. Visit your collection and aquarium

One regular fortune draw per day. New creatures are not guaranteed. Optional video ads offer redraws or extra pearls. Fortunes are for entertainment.
