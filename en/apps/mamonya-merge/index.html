Mamonya Merge

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Find your favorite part.

01

Make every angle count

Pull back, aim and release. Pick your landing spot to bring matching cats together.

02

Merge your way up

Matching Mamonya evolve when they collide. Plan the next shot and see how far your collection of shapes can grow.

03

Keep clear of the OUT line

Balance your board and chase a higher score. A cat falling below the OUT line ends the run.

GET STARTED

A few steps to get started.

  1. Pull back to aim
  2. Release toward a matching Mamonya
  3. Merge, evolve and keep the board in play

Game text: Japanese. Mamonya are fictional guardian cats; the character on their foreheads means “protect.”
