Kitchen Notes

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Find your favorite part.

01

Decide what to cook

Choose your ingredients, time and servings. Kitchen Notes suggests up to three options from recipes stored on your device.

02

Shop for the right amounts

Scale ingredients for the people you are cooking for, then save what you need in a shopping memo. Check items off as you shop.

03

Keep your place in the kitchen

Check off recipe steps and set cooking timers. Save recipes, add your food photos, and back up your collection.

GET STARTED

A few steps to get started.

  1. Choose ingredients, time and servings
  2. Pick a dish and check your shopping memo
  3. Follow the steps and start a timer

Dinner suggestions use saved recipe data. AI recipe creation happens in an external service; import its recipe JSON using the app’s prompt. Timer alerts depend on Android permissions, volume and device settings.
