About this website

This is SpringPort’s official app website. It has no analytics tags, advertising tags or contact forms. When you follow a link to Google Play or another site, that site’s policies apply.

Links to Google Play carry campaign tags to distinguish sources such as social profiles. Supported source and campaign identifiers are kept in sessionStorage for navigation within the same tab. If storage is unavailable, links carry the tags where possible. This website sends no analytics events to a server.
